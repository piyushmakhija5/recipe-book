## Ingredients
- 500 g bone-in chicken thighs
- 2 cups basmati rice, soaked 30 min
- 2 large onions, thinly sliced
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 3 tbsp biryani masala
- a pinch of saffron, soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk

## Steps
1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt and half the biryani masala for at least 20 minutes.
2. Fry the sliced onions until deep golden; set half aside for garnish.
3. Parboil the soaked rice with whole spices until about 70% cooked, then drain.
4. Layer the chicken, fried onions, and rice; finish with the saffron milk.
5. Cover and cook on low (dum) for 25 minutes, then rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes
- Swap the chicken for paneer or vegetables to make it vegetarian.
- A tight lid keeps the steam in during the dum stage.
